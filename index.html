About the Resort

Jacob Hotel & Spa is an elegant three-star hotel located along the pristine shores of Monk Street Beach in St. Catherine. The resort features 20 tastefully designed rooms, offering guests breathtaking Ocean View, serene Garden View and relaxing Sky View accommodation options.

Guests enjoy a range of facilities including spa services, dining options, beach access, free Wi-Fi, and professional customer service in a relaxing and secure environment.
