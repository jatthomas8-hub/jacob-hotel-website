About the Resort

Jacob Hotel & Spa is an elegant three-star retreat located along the pristine shores of Monk Street Beach in St. Catherine. Designed for guests who appreciate refined comfort and tranquil surroundings, the resort offers a peaceful escape where nature and modern hospitality blend seamlessly.

The resort features 20 tastefully designed rooms offering breathtaking Ocean View, serene Garden View, and relaxing Sky View accommodations. Each space is thoughtfully created to promote rest, privacy, and relaxation.

Guests are invited to unwind by the sparkling swimming pool, indulge in rejuvenating spa treatments, and enjoy local and international cuisine. With complimentary Wi-Fi, attentive service, and a secure environment, every stay is crafted to be memorable and effortlessly luxurious.
