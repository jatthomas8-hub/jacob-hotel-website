About the Resort

Jacob Hotel & Spa is an elegant three-star hotel located along the pristine shores of Monk Street Beach in St. Catherine. The resort features 20 tastefully designed rooms offering breathtaking Ocean View, serene Garden View, and relaxing Sky View accommodations.

Guests enjoy a tranquil atmosphere complemented by professional service, modern comforts, and premium facilities designed to create a truly memorable experience.
