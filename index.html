About the Resort

Jacob Hotel & Spa is an elegant three-star retreat located along the pristine shores of Monk Street Beach in St. Catherine. Designed for guests who value refined comfort and tranquil surroundings, the resort blends natural beauty with modern hospitality.

Our 20 tastefully designed rooms offer breathtaking Ocean View, serene Garden View, and calming Sky View accommodations, each created to promote rest, privacy, and relaxation.
